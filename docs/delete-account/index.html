Delete your ParkNext account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. There are two ways to do it, and both result in the same permanent deletion.

In the app

  1. Open ParkNext and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email service.parknext@gmail.com from the address on your ParkNext account, with the subject Delete my account.

We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply. If you write from a different address we will ask you to verify that the account is yours first, so that nobody else can delete it.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and about text
  • Every parking space you published, including its address and photos
  • Every booking you made or received
  • Every review and report you wrote
  • Your push notification tokens

This is permanent. Deletion is not a deactivation, and we cannot restore an account or its data afterwards. If you only want to stop receiving booking requests, set your listings to inactive instead — that leaves your account intact.

What may remain

A booking you were party to continues to exist for the other person, since it is their record of the arrangement too, but it no longer carries your name or contact details. We may also keep records we are legally required to retain, for as long as the law requires.

The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
