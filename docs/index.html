For drivers

Three taps from circling
the block to parked.

No hunting, no meter, no attendant. You see what is free right now, you book it, and the host lets you in with a code.

01

See what is actually free

A live map of spaces around you, with the price, the photos and whether it is covered, guarded or has a charger — before you commit to driving there.

02

Book now, or book ahead

Need it this minute, or a slot on Thursday at ten? Pick the window and only spaces genuinely open then will show up.

03

Arrive with a code

Show the host your four digits. They type it in, your timer starts, and the space is yours until you tap that you are leaving.

The handover

Four digits instead
of a barrier.

Your booking does not start when you book it. It starts the moment the host types your code — so nobody pays for time they did not get, and nobody holds a space for someone who never turned up.

Leaving early ends the booking there and then, and puts the rest of the slot back on the map for someone else.

Everything in the box

Built for the ten minutes
you would rather not spend.

Live map

Real GPS, real availability, OpenStreetMap under the hood. Filter by price band, amenities or vehicle type.

Hourly, or scheduled

Instant bookings for right now; pick a day, start time and duration for anything later.

A timer you can extend

Watch the time left count down, and add fifteen or thirty minutes without walking back to the car.

Two-way ratings

Drivers rate hosts, hosts rate drivers. The whole trust system, since there is no money in the middle.

You pay the host

Cash or UPI, directly, at the space. ParkNext takes no commission and never sees a card number.

Contacts stay shut

Phone numbers only unlock once a booking is accepted — enforced in the database, not just hidden on a screen.
